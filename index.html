A journal, not a to-do list

Thirty things, before thirty.

— days to go

Sometime around the end of 2027, I turn thirty. Between now and then I'm chasing thirty specific things — a mix of travel, money, health, and the people who matter — and keeping the story here as it unfolds, mostly for the people who'll ask "so did you ever...?"

None of these are resolutions in the New Year's sense — no vague "get healthier" or "travel more." Each one is specific enough to actually finish, and specific enough that I can't quietly forget I said it. Some are big, some are small, all of them are things I'd regret not doing on purpose.

I'm keeping score privately, but sharing the list itself here — partly for accountability, partly because a few of these (the trip, the language, the mentor) are more fun with people cheering from the sidelines.

I

Travel & Adventure

Get further from home than comfortable, more than once.
  • Take a solo trip somewhere I've never been
  • Get certified in something adventurous — scuba, tandem skydiving, or a wilderness course
  • Sleep somewhere with zero light pollution and see the Milky Way
  • Take an overnight train or ferry to a new city
  • Visit a continent I've never set foot on
  • Go on a trip with no fixed itinerary, at least three days long
  • Do one "type 2 fun" adventure — brutal in the moment, great in the retelling
  • Spend a full weekend off-grid with no phone signal
  • Navigate a foreign city on public transit alone, in a language I don't speak
II

Career & Money

Build the stability and momentum that free up everything else on this list.
  • Build a 3–6 month emergency fund and leave it alone
  • Negotiate a raise, a promotion, or a new offer
  • Ship a side project from idea to something real, however small
  • Earn a certification or credential that moves my career forward
  • Track every dollar I spend for one full month
  • Ask someone I admire to mentor me, formally or not
  • Turn down one opportunity that doesn't fit where I'm going
III

Health & Body

Make the body last for the other seventy-plus years.
  • Run a 5K, 10K, or half marathon
  • Get a full physical and know my actual numbers
  • Keep a workout routine consistent for six straight months
  • Complete a 30-day challenge of my own choosing
  • Fix one specific sleep habit and track it for a month
  • Cook most of my meals at home for a month straight
  • Go a full week with no screens after dinner
IV

People & Growth

Do right by the people who matter, and become someone slightly braver.
  • Reconnect with someone I've lost touch with
  • Learn a language to real conversational level
  • Volunteer consistently for something I care about, not just once
  • Start a journaling habit and keep it
  • Do one thing that genuinely scares me, on purpose
  • Take a class purely for fun, with zero career angle
  • Spend real one-on-one time with each parent or sibling, not just group time

This page will update as the list moves — no need to check a spreadsheet, just come back. And if you want to cheer me on, tell me I'm wrong about one of these, or hold me to it, leave a comment below.

— Tao
